About

One lab identity for research across emerging network domains.

LUMEN-X stands for Low-power, Ultra-reliable, Multidomain and Emerging Networks eXploration.

Mission

The lab develops rigorous and deployable networking solutions that improve reliability, responsiveness, energy efficiency, scalability and security across heterogeneous communication systems.

Research approach

Our work combines analytical modelling, protocol and algorithm design, simulation, testbed experimentation, data-driven adaptation and application-oriented evaluation.

Institutional affiliation

LUMEN-X Lab is led by Prof. Nikumani Choudhury at the Department of Computer Science and Information Systems, BITS Pilani, Hyderabad Campus.

What LUMEN-X represents

  • Low-power and resource-aware design
  • Ultra-reliable communication
  • Multidomain integration
  • Emerging network architectures
  • Experimental and analytical exploration
