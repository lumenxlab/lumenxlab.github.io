About

Raziur Rahman is a joint doctoral researcher at BITS Pilani, Hyderabad Campus, and La Trobe University through the Asian Smart Cities Research and Innovation Network (ASCRIN). His research focuses on adaptive scheduling and resource allocation in 6TiSCH-based IoT networks for smart-city applications, with the objective of developing scalable, energy-efficient, and reliable communication mechanisms for dynamic and bursty traffic.

Supervisors

Prof. Nikumani Choudhury, BITS Pilani Hyderabad Campus, and Prof. Wei Xiang, La Trobe University.
