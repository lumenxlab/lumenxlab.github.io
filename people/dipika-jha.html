About

Dipika Jha is a joint doctoral researcher at BITS Pilani, Hyderabad Campus, and La Trobe University. Her research focuses on artificial intelligence and machine learning for communication and networked systems.

Supervisors

Prof. Jay Kamlesh Dave and Prof. Peng Cheng are supervisors; Prof. Nikumani Choudhury and Dr Lydia Cui are co-supervisors.

Research Focus Contact

Research Interests and Technical Focus

  • Artificial intelligence for networked systems
  • Machine learning for communication networks
  • Data-driven network optimisation
  • Intelligent and adaptive communication systems
