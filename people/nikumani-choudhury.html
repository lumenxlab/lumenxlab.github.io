About

Prof. Nikumani Choudhury is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Information Systems at BITS Pilani, Hyderabad Campus. He is an IEEE Senior Member and serves as Principal Investigator and Head of LUMEN-X Lab. His research spans low-power IoT networks, LoRa and LoRaWAN, IEEE 802.15.4-based communication, private 5G networks, resource allocation, adaptive scheduling and optical quantum communication.
