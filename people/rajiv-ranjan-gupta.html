Research Profile

Research Topic

Secured & Intelligent Vehicular Transportation Framework for Indo-Australian Smart Cities

Short Bio

Rajiv Ranjan Gupta is a PhD researcher in the BITS Pilani–RMIT University joint program. He is supervised by Prof. Ke Wang of RMIT University and Prof. Nikumani Choudhury of BITS Pilani, with additional supervision from Prof. Saman Atapattu of RMIT University and Prof. Jay Kamlesh Dave of BITS Pilani.

His research focuses on a secured and intelligent vehicular transportation framework for Indo-Australian smart cities. His work spans intelligent transportation systems and V2X communication for autonomous driving, emphasizing reliable, low-latency and secure information exchange for resource-constrained edge and ITS deployments.

He holds a Master’s degree in Design Engineering from BITS Pilani. After completing his M.Tech, he worked as an Engineer at Innominds Software Pvt. Ltd. He later served as an Assistant Professor at BITS Pilani, where he taught and mentored students in connected and autonomous vehicles, advanced driver-assistance systems, vehicular networks, automotive cybersecurity, artificial intelligence and deep learning.

He has also led industry–academia programmes involving TATA Motors, Bosch and Hyundai Mobis.

Research Objective

This research investigates advanced vehicle-to-everything communication networks for intelligent transportation systems and autonomous-driving applications. It focuses on improving communication reliability, resource efficiency, security and network performance in highly dynamic vehicular environments.

The work also explores new and less congested higher-frequency spectrum bands to address bandwidth limitations and spectrum scarcity in conventional vehicular communication systems. Technologies such as Cellular V2X, Optical Camera Communication, millimetre-wave communication and hybrid RF-optical frameworks are considered to improve connectivity and support next-generation intelligent mobility solutions.

Projects, Tools and Experimental Platforms

  • MATLAB for communication-system modelling, algorithm development and performance analysis.
  • Python for data analysis, protocol evaluation and experimental processing.
  • WaveForms for hardware-oriented measurements, signal observation and experimental studies.
  • Simulation of Urban MObility, or SUMO, for real-time vehicular mobility and traffic-scenario generation.
  • Simulation and experimental evaluation of V2X, ITS, Optical Camera Communication and hybrid RF-optical communication systems.
