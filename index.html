About LUMEN-X

Research spanning constrained devices, intelligent infrastructure and future networks.

The lab provides a common research identity for scholars working across IoT, wireless networking, multidomain communication, security and quantum networking.

Our mission

To develop rigorous and deployable communication solutions that improve reliability, responsiveness, energy efficiency and security across heterogeneous network domains.

Our work connects theoretical models with protocol implementation, simulation, testbed experimentation and application-driven evaluation.

01

Low-power by design

Resource-aware communication for constrained IoT, IIoT, WSN, LPWAN and cyber-physical systems.

02

Reliability with measurable guarantees

Deterministic networking, adaptive scheduling, QoS optimization and resilient protocol operation.

03

Multidomain integration

Terrestrial, aerial, satellite, optical, underwater, classical and quantum communication domains.

04

Emerging network intelligence

Edge intelligence, AI-driven networking, distributed control, anomaly detection and next-generation architectures.

Research themes

Four connected pillars under one multidomain vision.

These pillars retain a coherent networking identity while accommodating the lab’s current work and future research directions.

LP

Low-Power and Deterministic IoT

Protocols and resource-control mechanisms for reliable, energy-efficient and time-sensitive communication in constrained and industrial networks.

  • IEEE 802.15.4e
  • TSCH
  • 6TiSCH
  • DSME
  • LPWAN
  • Scheduling
AI

Mobile and Intelligent Networks

Adaptive, learning-assisted and edge-enabled networking for dynamic traffic, mobile nodes and distributed cyber-physical infrastructure.

  • Mobile IoT
  • Edge Intelligence
  • AI-driven Networking
  • IoV & V2X
  • UAV Networks
  • Fog Computing
MD

Multidomain Communication Systems

Network design across terrestrial, non-terrestrial, aerial, optical and underwater communication environments, including hybrid RF–optical systems.

  • 5G / 6G
  • Private 5G
  • NTN
  • Optical Networks
  • Underwater Networks
  • Hybrid Networks
SQ

Secure and Quantum Networking

Authentication, cyber-physical security and quantum communication mechanisms for resilient and future-ready network infrastructures.

  • IoT Security
  • Authentication
  • QKD
  • Quantum Routing
  • Quantum Repeaters
  • Hybrid Quantum Networks
